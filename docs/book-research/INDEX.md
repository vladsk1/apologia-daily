# Book-research — topical index (consult this BEFORE drafting any resurrection / deity / apologetics content)

**Purpose.** This folder holds in-our-own-words maps of *owned copyrighted* apologetics books. This
index routes a topic → the note(s) + section + the strongest **already-identified primary sources** to
cite. **Workflow for any content session (essay, `ev-s*` card, `/answers/*`):** before drafting on one of
these topics, `Grep`/`Read` the mapped note, take the *argument shape + the primaries to cite*, then quote
the **primaries** (verified), never the book's prose.

**Hard rules (see `README.md`):** every citation in these notes is an **unverified lead** until confirmed
against the primary and run through `apologia-citations → apologia-argument → apologia-orthodoxy`.
Copyrighted-book text never enters `/sources` or a live answer. The live `api/ask.js` endpoint does **not**
read this folder (it can't — not deployed/served, and these are unverified leads); the only path from
these books to a *live* answer is: lead → verify the primary → add it to `/sources` as `verified:true`.

## ⚠ Standing "do not use / high-caution" flags (carry these into any pull)
- 🔴🔴 **RotGH — THREE QUOTATIONS IN THIS BOOK ARE PRESENTED AS CONCESSIONS AND ARE NOT.** (a) **George
  Greenstein** (p. 145) poses the "supernatural Agency" question rhetorically in *The Symbiotic Universe*
  and then **rejects** that answer — the most notorious quote-mine in the fine-tuning literature. (b)
  **Dawkins in *Expelled*** (p. 181) offered a designer as a **reductio**, saying immediately that such a
  designer would itself have to have evolved; the film's interviewees also say they were solicited under a
  different description. (c) **Crick's "almost a miracle"** (p. 181) is a statement of the problem's
  difficulty by a man who spent his career pursuing naturalistic answers to it. **Use none of the three.**
  For "the origin of life is genuinely unsolved," the same chapter supplies far better sources in their own
  published words: **Christian de Duve, Dobzhansky, Prigogine.** Related: **Brian Josephson** (p. 145) is a
  Nobel laureate who also endorses telepathy and cold fusion — a liability, not an asset; and two of ch. 7's
  four testimonials are **Meyer's own unpublished interviews**, as is the George Ellis line at p. 122.
- 🔴🔴 **RotGH ch. 7 — THE BOOK IS LESS ACCURATE THAN OUR OWN CERTIFIED ESSAY, AND MINING IT WOULD PUT BACK
  A CLAIM WE RETIRED.** Meyer tells the carbon-resonance story anthropically — Hoyle predicted the 7.65 MeV
