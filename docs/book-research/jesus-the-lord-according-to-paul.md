## Book thesis + structure (from Preface, pp. xvii–xx — in our own words)
- Fee's aim: a careful, accessible analysis of **the person of Christ in Paul** — *who* Christ is and *why he
  came* — distilled from the 2007 *Pauline Christology* for a non-specialist readership.
- **Two primary christological emphases** the book pursues (Preface):
  1. **Preexistence → Incarnation** (Parts 1–2): the divine Son must be understood as genuinely **incarnate**;
     Paul assumes Christ **lived a truly human life** ("on our planet"). Preexistence-as-God entails the Son
     within the **divine identity**.
  2. **Second Adam / *eikōn* ("image")** (Part 2): Christ as the true image of God (Gen 1–2), **restoring the
     divine image lost in the fall**; salvation's goal is being re-created into that image.
- **Parts 3–4** (the "Jewish Messiah" emphases): Jesus as **Son of God** / Son of David (Part 3), and as the
  now-exalted **"Lord"** — the *Kyrios* of **Ps 110:1**, where Paul applies to Jesus the **LXX substitute for
  Yahweh** (Part 4).
- **Conclusion — "Paul as a Proto-trinitarian":** Paul remains a **monotheistic Jew by presupposition**, yet
  attributes to Christ activities/identity that (in Fee's phrase) belong to God alone — so the relationship of
  the Son to the Father raises the Trinity. ✅ **This is the site's `jesus_as_god_nt` / `shema` thesis
  exactly** (monotheism redefined around Christ) — a strong corroboration candidate and possibly a
  better-articulated frame for the missing "Did Paul invent Jesus's divinity?" essay. Confirm at Step Zero.
- Fee on **1 Cor 1:18–2:5** (Preface): Paul's "demolition" of the Corinthians' boasted wisdom — **God's
  "wisdom" is the crucified Messiah** ("God's foolishness"), not a personified Wisdom figure (see Flag 3).
