# Scholarly book wishlist

*Written 2026-09-24. Which owned books would most improve the site, in priority order. Titles and
years are from general knowledge; confirm the edition before buying. Buy only copies you own
outright (print or Kindle). Never Perlego.*

Each book, once owned, goes through `docs/book-research/README.md`: Step Zero (read the paired essays
in full first), an our-own-words note, the six-verdict cross-check, then backlog rows.

**Why owning matters even for books we already cite.** Our essays cite several of these repeatedly
(Wright ×20+, Hurtado ×10+, the *Blackwell Companion* ×8+, Bauckham), but we have **no research note
on any of them**, and `CLAUDE.md` lists "~a dozen CHECK-level page-cites" that need a human with
the physical book. Owning them lets us verify page numbers and quotations, and deepen the essays.

## Buying advice
- **Start with 1–7.** They support pages already live, and they settle page-citation checks we owe.
- **Then 14–16.** These fill the biggest topic gaps.
- **Use Kindle deal sites** (see the deal-sources list from 2026-09-24: Challies, Gospel eBooks, the
  "Kindle Deals in … Apologetics" page). Academic titles (Blackwell, Oxford, Cambridge) rarely hit $2.99
  but do drop; used print via ThriftBooks is often cheapest.
- **Rough cost (estimate):** Tier 1 about US$200–350 total; all 30 about US$800–1,300 (new; less used or on sale).
