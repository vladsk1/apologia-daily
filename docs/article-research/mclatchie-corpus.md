## Thesis / distinctives (our words)

McLatchie's apologetic signature is **Bayesian, evidence-maximalist New Testament apologetics** plus
**Discovery-Institute intelligent-design biology**. Four strands matter for us:

1. **Resurrection — the "MAXIMAL DATA" approach, explicitly AGAINST the minimal-facts method.** This is
   his most distinctive contribution relative to our site. He is a *critic of the minimal-facts strategy*
   (Habermas/Licona) and argues for a broader "maximal data" case that leans on the Gospels' and Acts'
   demonstrated reliability rather than restricting itself to what the critical majority already grants.
   His stated worry (shared with the McGrews' probabilistic school and popularizers such as the "Cerebral
   Faith" blog — ⚠ do not attribute to specific named individuals unverified):
   if a sceptic can grant every minimal fact and still rationally decline the conclusion, the method has
   conceded too much of its own evidence base. He runs the case Bayesianly (the resurrection as an
   *authenticating sign* has a high likelihood on theism-plus-Jesus'-claims) and via a
   disciples'-belief trilemma (honest mistake / deliberate deception / real appearance).
2. **Undesigned coincidences** (a genuine McLatchie specialty) — he has a multi-part "Yes, Richard
   Carrier, There Are Undesigned Coincidences" series and further replies (to Dan McClellan, John
   Nelson, Kurt Jaros). **Our `coincidences.html` already cites this series** (fn 18 + bibliography).
3. **Islam** — a large response corpus, including a multi-part reply to Abu Zakariya's *Jesus: Man,
   Messenger, Messiah*, "A Simple Reason Why the Qur'an Cannot Be the Word of God," the **"crucifixion
   dilemma,"** and **"How Does Paul's Relationship with the Jerusalem Church Present a Problem for Islam?"**
4. **Intelligent design / biology** (Discovery Institute; irreducible complexity; the *BIO-Complexity*
   paper). **SENSITIVE — full-essay-only, honest-inference framing, never a card; do NOT import as a gap.**
