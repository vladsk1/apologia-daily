# Mining brief — NT scholars & journals directory (2026-09-18)

> **What this is.** A durable, refreshed reference directory of the best New Testament scholars and the
> peer-reviewed journals/books where their work lives — **with legal-access routes** — mapped to our
> Evidence Library and our real coverage gaps. It **consolidates and refreshes**
> [`MINING-BRIEF-scholar-source-hunt-2026-09.md`](MINING-BRIEF-scholar-source-hunt-2026-09.md) (the
> 2026-09-04 pass, whose audit found the library mature and logged 8 backlog rows), **re-verifies its key
> free links**, and **adds scholars/journals/gaps it missed**. Use it as the "who's who + where's it free"
> map a future content session consults alongside the topic routers in `INDEX.md`,
> `book-research/INDEX.md`, and `video-research/INDEX.md`.
>
> **⚠ EVERYTHING HERE IS AN UNVERIFIED LEAD.** Per the project rules, no scholar, journal, book title,
> URL, or OA claim below may be cited in live content until the *primary* is confirmed and run through
> `apologia-citations → apologia-argument → apologia-orthodoxy`. We **never** cite "an article said X" —
> we cite the **primary** it points to, verified. A prior session fabricated a citation; do not invent.
>
> **Access flags used below:**
> - **FREE ✅** = an open-access / author-hosted / permission-aggregator / SEP-IEP route whose URL was
>   **verified to resolve this session** (2026-09-18) via WebSearch. (WebFetch to primary hosts is
>   egress-blocked in this sandbox — the same limitation the SWEEP_HANDOVER records — so verification was
>   by search-resolution of the canonical URL + the site's own OA statement, not by fetching the PDF.)
> - **FREE (std)** = a well-established OA/author host **not re-fetched this session** (egress-blocked),
>   carried from the 2026-09-04 brief; re-confirm on use.
> - **ACQUIRE** = genuinely not free online (buy / library) but high-value.
> - **UNVERIFIED** = could not confirm a legal free route; treat as ACQUIRE until confirmed.
> - ⚠ For any `academia.edu` / `ResearchGate` hit, **confirm it is the author's OWN upload** before use —
>   never a third-party re-host. **Sci-Hub / pirate mirrors: never.**

## Standing guardrails to carry into every recommendation (do not drop these)
- **Manuscripts prove accurate *preservation*, not the *truth* of the content** — argue truth separately
  (`manuscript`, `deadseascrolls`).
- **Fine-tuning:** the *data* is conceded even by skeptics; *design* is the contested inference — never
  "scientists agree it's designed" (`finetuning`, `cosmic`, `privileged`). ⚠ **Lewis = multiverse
  advocate, Barnes = theist** (the guardrail was once backwards on-site — see backlog row 244).
- **Resurrection leads with the early 1 Cor 15:3–7 creed**, never "written decades later."
- **Denominational neutrality** — do not adjudicate Catholic/Orthodox/Protestant disputes (canon *scope*/
  Apocrypha, Thomism-as-"the" view, ID + young/old-earth in the Science tab, baptism, papacy, Marian
  doctrine, eschatology).
- **NTWrightPage screen** — mine Wright's resurrection + Second-Temple historiography ONLY; do **not**
  import his justification / New-Perspective-on-Paul distinctives into our neutral voice.
- **⚠⚠⚠ Bauckham universalism fence** — Richard Bauckham holds universalist views that fall outside what
  this site may affirm. **Mine ONLY his historiography** (eyewitnesses, Papias, onomastics, the *inclusio*,
  divine-identity Christology as a historical claim). Cite him **as a historian on a historical question**,
  never as a theological authority, never in a further-reading list without naming the specific work.
- **Islam** — represent charitably, then LAND the explicit bounded verdict; stay clear of the **RETIRED**
  lexical "Word of God" bridge (`tools/retired-claims.json`) — use `islam-eternalword.html`'s STRUCTURAL
  framing.
- **Retired claim:** never revive "the Daniel 7 figure is divine / worshipped" (`daniel7-figure-is-divine`,
  `pelach-only-to-God`).
- **Tier discipline:** popular/ministry sites (str.org, apologetics315, Justin Taylor's TGC blog) and
  student journals (Eleutheria) are **pointers to primaries, not authorities to cite**. Even peer-reviewed
  sources carry tilt — hold every pull to the `CLAUDE.md` argument-specific rules.

### A1. Early high Christology / divine-identity (deity of Christ, Trinity NT-case)
*This is the mainstream scholarly route to an **early, high** Christology — the case our deity/Trinity
cluster rests on. It is **already load-bearing on-site**: Bauckham appears in 21 essays, Hurtado in 14 (see
the saturation table in Section C).*

  (`ridley.cam.ac.uk/team/professor-richard-bauckham`). Divine-identity Christology (*Jesus and the God of
  Israel* / *God Crucified*) + eyewitness historiography. **Best free entry point: FREE ✅
  `richardbauckham.co.uk/articles/` and `/publications/`** — ⭐ **the broken-TLS problem the 2026-09-04
  brief flagged is RESOLVED; the site now resolves cleanly.** ⚠⚠⚠ **universalism fence** — historiography
  only. Books = ACQUIRE.

  `larryhurtado.wordpress.com`** — the blog is kept online as an archive, with a **"List of Selected
  Publications"** page and posted essays. Plus the Tyndale Bulletin PDF "The Origins of Jesus-Devotion,"
  *TynBul* 60.2 (2009). Books = ACQUIRE.
