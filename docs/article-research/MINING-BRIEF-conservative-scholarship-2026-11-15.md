> **What this is.** A ranked list of the **journals** worth mining long-term and the **specific articles** to mine
> next, aimed at the gaps the site actually has. It is the fourth source hunt. It does **not** repeat the

> gate. Nothing here has been mined yet.
>
> **Licence shorthand.** **HOST** = public domain or CC BY (the site may carry text). **LINK** = free to read
> but copyrighted (summarise in our own words, cite the primaries; the site is commercial).

## Part A — Free accounts worth making (owner action)

| Account | What it unlocks | Worth it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Public / state library card** | Many libraries give cardholders remote access to **EBSCO, ProQuest, JSTOR and sometimes the ATLA Religion Database** (the main theology index, with full text of WTJ, BBR and others). LA and New York public libraries are confirmed to carry ATLA; others vary. If you're in Australia, check your **state library's** free membership and its eResources list (not verified for any specific library). | ⭐ **Best single step.** It reaches the paid journals legally. |
| **JSTOR personal account** | Free online reading. ⚠ JSTOR's own announcement says that **from 1 Sept 2026 the free limit is 10 articles per 30 days** (down from the pandemic-era 100); some help pages still say 100. Read-online only, and not every article qualifies. | Yes, for a few key articles a month (e.g. Marilyn McCord Adams, Wykstra). |
| **Academia.edu / ResearchGate** | Downloads of scholars' **own** uploads. | Yes, but use **only the author's own upload**. Never a third party's copy. |
| **BiblicalTraining.org** | Free seminary-level lecture courses (Kruger on canon, and others). | Yes. Treat lectures as video leads (`docs/video-research/` rules). |
| **EPS (Evangelical Philosophical Society)** | Free web papers. *Philosophia Christi*'s full archive (1999–) is **paid**: about $25/yr on top of membership. | The free papers, yes. The paid archive is optional, but it is the best evangelical philosophy journal. |
| No account needed | PhilPapers/PhilArchive, Project MUSE open-access books, Tyndale Bulletin, JETS, Themelios, SEP/IEP. | Use freely. |

**Never:** Perlego (forbidden by `CLAUDE.md`), Sci-Hub, LibGen, Scribd uploads of other people's PDFs,
"free PDF" mirror sites, or Internet Archive loans of in-copyright books (that lending lost in court in
*Hachette v. Internet Archive*). Its public-domain scans are fine.

### Tier 3: useful archives with conditions
- **biblicalstudies.org.uk** — huge, legal archive (Evangelical Quarterly, Churchman, Trinity Journal, older
  Tyndale Bulletin). **Check each article's permission line:** items over 70 years old are public domain;
  newer ones are hosted by permission, so they are **LINK**, not HOST.
- **Perspectives on Science and Christian Faith** (ASA) — full archive on asa3.org. ⚠ The ASA spans
  evolutionary creationists and old-earth views. Use it for science-history only, never to settle
  origins debates (the science-tab neutrality concern in `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Zygon** (open access since 2024, back archive CC BY) — already flagged in the 2026-10-04 brief as the best
  science-history source. Not conservative; use it for facts, not framing.

### ⚠ Tradition fences (read before mining any of these)
Seminary journals are confessional. MSJ is cessationist and dispensational, SBJT is Baptist, WTJ is
Reformed, and Nova et Vetera is Catholic Thomist. **Mine them for the faith all these traditions share
(resurrection, deity, reliability, natural theology), never for baptism, election, end times, gifts,
sacraments or the papacy.** For neutrality, balance an evangelical lead with a Catholic or Orthodox one
where possible.

## Not recommended

- **Popular apologetics websites as sources.** CARM, GotQuestions and similar sites are fine for finding
  topics, never for citing. We cite the primaries.
- **Young-earth-creationist journals** (*Answers Research Journal*, *Journal of Creation*). These take sides
  in an intra-Christian dispute the site deliberately stays out of, and `is-god-real.md` already flagged CMI
  quote-mining as do-not-use.
- **BIO-Complexity** (intelligent design, open access). Usable only with the science-tab neutrality fence.
  Design remains the contested inference.
