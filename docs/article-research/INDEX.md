# Article-research — topical index (consult this BEFORE drafting; sibling of `book-research/INDEX.md` and `video-research/INDEX.md`)

**Purpose.** This folder holds in-our-own-words maps of *copyrighted modern apologetics journal
articles and essays*. This index is the **"what's the best source for this topic?"** router for a
content session: topic → the note(s) + section + the strongest **already-identified primary sources**
to cite.

**Workflow for any content session (essay, `ev-s*` card, `/answers/*`):** before drafting on one of
these topics, `Grep`/`Read` the mapped note, take the *argument shape + the primaries to cite*, then
quote the **primaries** (verified) — never the article. Consult this **alongside**
`book-research/INDEX.md`, `video-research/INDEX.md`, and the `/sources` corpus, and use whichever has
the best material for the topic.

**Hard rules (see `README.md`):** every citation in these notes is an **unverified lead** until
confirmed against the primary and run through `apologia-citations → apologia-argument →
apologia-orthodoxy`. The article's text never enters `/sources` (PD-verbatim only) or a live answer,
and we never cite "an article/essay said X" — we cite the **primary** it pointed us to, verified. Use
only genuinely-legal free sources (open-access / author-hosted / permission-based aggregators / SEP-IEP);
never piracy; academia.edu/ResearchGate only if it's the author's own upload. The live `api/ask.js`
endpoint does **not** read this folder. An article reaches a *live* answer only through the two gated
doors any research lead uses: (1) lead → verify a **public-domain** primary → `/sources` as
`verified:true`; or (2) lead → verified primaries → a **certified essay** → a gated **`/briefs`** entry.

## ⚠ Standing "do not use / high-caution" flags (carry these into any pull)
- **Even peer-reviewed sources carry tilt.** Hold every pull to the `CLAUDE.md` argument-specific
  rules (Kalam "begins to exist"; fine-tuning *data* conceded / *design* the contested inference;
  resurrection leads with the early creed; morality = duties-need-a-ground; manuscripts prove
  preservation not truth; "orthodoxy outranks charity").
- **Denominational tilt to screen:** NTWrightPage (do **not** import justification / New-Perspective
  distinctives); str.org & Detroit Baptist Seminary Journal (Protestant/Baptist tilt — screen
  intra-Christian disputes per neutrality); any Reformed/Catholic/dispensational distinctive stays out
  of our neutral voice.
- **Tier / authority:** Eleutheria (student scholarship) and str.org (popular) are **pointers, not
  authorities** — use them to find primaries, not as the citation.
- **Access discipline:** Philosophia Christi & Bulletin for Biblical Research are **free-portion only**;
  never cite from behind a paywall you didn't legally access. Sci-Hub is piracy — never.
- **SEP/IEP steelman hard.** Great for stating the argument in its strongest current form and for
  bibliographies — but land our own verdict per the explicit-verdict rule; don't inherit their agnostic
  framing as our voice.
- **Never cite the article.** If a claim appears only in the essay, it isn't verified — find the primary
  or drop it.
