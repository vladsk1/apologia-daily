## Executable rows (→ `docs/content-backlog.md`)
- **Row 1 (P2, HIGH) — Allison's own concession.** The strongest living critic: "I am sure that the disciples saw Jesus after his death" (*Resurrecting Jesus*, T&T Clark 2005, **346**, cf. 343), and grants the NT reports — Paul's included — are of a **bodily** Jesus (**317, 324–325**); quoted in Habermas 2008: 306, 312. Add to the firewall paragraph. ⚠ STEELMAN: "grants the disciples saw a bodily-appearing Jesus, undecided on the cause" — NEVER "Allison affirms the resurrection" or "Habermas refuted Allison" (Allison keeps the naturalistic door open, 334).
- **Row 2 (P3, MED) — appearances are a closed category, not open-ended visions.** The NT distinguishes the resurrection *appearances* from later visions of Jesus (Stephen; Acts 22:17–18 given no "appearance" status) and they ceased (~40 days, Acts 1:3) — unlike open-ended visionary experience. Primary: **Theodor Keim, *History of Jesus of Nazara* (1873–83), 6:353** (PUBLIC DOMAIN — and Keim *rejected* the empty tomb, so not question-begging); Acts 22:17–18; Acts 1:3. Via Habermas 2008: 309. New `<h3>` after "Visions do not empty tombs." ⚠ Bounded: shows the reports don't behave like open-ended visions, not a proof.
- **Row 3 (P3, LOW-MED) — Paul's cross-check.** Paul confirms his gospel's *content* matched the other apostles' proclamation of the appearances: **Gal 1:18–19; 2:1–10; 1 Cor 15:11** ("whether I or they, so we preach"). Add to the independence paragraph. ⚠ Modest claim.
- **Row 4 (P4, LOW) — Paul's glorified blinding-light phenomenology.** His risen Jesus was a glorified light "brighter than the sun" that blinded him (**Acts 26:13; 9:8–9; 22:11**) — the opposite of a grief-projection of the earthly Jesus. ⚠ Do NOT press the companions' shared perception (Acts 9:7 vs 22:9 crux); use only the uncontested blinding light.
