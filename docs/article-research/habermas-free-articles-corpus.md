## Articles on the index that are NOT yet mapped (candidates for a future dedicated mining note)

Each URL below was surfaced by WebSearch on the `garyhabermas.com/articles/` domain; **treat as unverified
until fetched.** Cross-check verdicts are provisional (the articles could not be read this session).

1. ⭐ **"Recent Perspectives on the Reliability of the Gospels"** · *Christian Research Journal* 28.1 (2005) ·
   `garyhabermas.com/articles/crj_recentperspectives/crj_recentperspectives.htm` (also free at equip.org,
   bethinking.org, Liberty DigitalCommons). **Thesis (reported):** general defences of NT reliability are
   weak; surveys the *specific* recent critical paths — the criteria of authenticity applied to individual
   Gospel sayings/texts — and how they cut. **Cross-check verdict: GENUINELY NET-NEW topic for our Habermas
   notes** (none of the six touches Gospel-reliability method / criteria of authenticity). Would strengthen
   **`library/eyewitnesses.html`, `library/multiatt.html`, `library/manuscript.html`** and the deity-from-
   the-Gospels essays. ⚠ Not yet mined — the specific improvement can't be named until the article is read.
   **This is the top future-mining candidate.**

2. **"Experiences of the Risen Jesus: The Foundational Historical Issue in the Early Proclamation of the
   Resurrection"** · *Dialog: A Journal of Theology* 45.3 (2006): 288–297 ·
   `garyhabermas.com/articles/dialog_rexperience/dialog_rexperiences.htm`. **Thesis (reported):** the
   disciples' appearance-*experiences* are the single most-conceded, foundational datum of the resurrection
   debate — the plank the whole minimal-facts case pivots on. **Cross-check verdict: LIKELY CORROBORATION-
   HEAVY** — this is precisely the "what even the sceptics grant" material `library/minimalfacts.html` (fn
   14–18) and `library/appearances.html` already carry well. Worth reading for a possible *wording*
   enrichment, but expect verdict 1/5. MEDIUM priority.

3. **"The Late Twentieth-Century Resurgence of Naturalistic Responses to Jesus' Resurrection"** (a.k.a.
   "Responses to Jesus' Resurrection") · *Trinity Journal* 22 NS (2001): 179–196 ·
   `garyhabermas.com/articles/trinityjournal_latetwentieth/trinityjournal_latetwentieth.htm`. **Thesis
   (reported):** a catalogue/taxonomy of the naturalistic theories revived in late-20th-c. scholarship.
   **Cross-check verdict: SUBSTANTIAL OVERLAP with the already-mapped 2001 CRJ hallucination article and the
   2008 Allison critique** (`habermas-allison-hallucination-appearances.md`), and with `appearances.html`.
   Read it only to confirm it adds no *new* theory the mapped notes miss. MEDIUM-LOW priority.

4. **"The Resurrection of Jesus: A Rational Inquiry"** — Habermas's 1976 doctoral dissertation (Michigan
   State) · free PDF at `garyhabermas.com/books/dissertation/habermas_dissertation_1976.pdf`. **Verdict:
   FOUNDATIONAL, LOW priority** — historical interest; its case is fully superseded by the 2005/2012 articles
   and the 2024–25 volumes we already track. Not a mining target.

5. **"Resurrection"** — encyclopedia entry, *Wiley-Blackwell Encyclopedia of Christian Civilization* (2011).
   **Verdict: reference-tier, LOW** — a summary, not a source of new primaries.
