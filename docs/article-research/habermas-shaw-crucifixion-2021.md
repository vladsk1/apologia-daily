## Executable rows
- **Row 1 (P2) — the Josephus three-friends datum → `minimalfacts.html` swoon section.** ✅ **SHIPPED 2026-09-18.** Josephus (siege of Jerusalem) recognised three crucified acquaintances, got them released by **Titus**, taken down + given the best medical care — yet **two of the three died**. A concrete historical rebuttal to the swoon theory (deliberate rescue + physicians usually still failed). Primary: **Josephus, *The Life* 420–421** (Whiston). Cited via reused fn 8.
- **Row 2 (P3) — page-pin the Strauss quote → `minimalfacts.html` fn 9.** ✅ **SHIPPED 2026-09-18.** Our fn 9 admitted it couldn't page-pin the "half-dead / Prince of Life" quote; A35 (n. 26) gives **Strauss, *A New Life of Jesus* (Williams & Norgate, 1879), vol. 1, 412**. Added.
- **Row 3 (P3, DEFERRED) — Quintilian death-assurance / the spear-thrust → `minimalfacts.html` swoon OR `library/burial.html`.** Quintilian (*Declamationes maiores* 6:9): after crucifixion, bodies that were "struck/pierced" (*percussus* implies a weapon) could be buried — a Roman death-assurance practice that **corroborates John 19:33–35** (the spear-thrust). Brown, *Death of the Messiah* 2:1177–78, and Dunn, *Jesus Remembered* 1:781, cite Quintilian for the same. Strengthens "Jesus really died." ⚠ Verify Quintilian *Decl. mai.* 6:9 + Brown 2:1177–78. **OPEN.**
- **Row 4 (P3, DEFERRED) — burial of crucifixion victims → `library/burial.html` / `library/emptytomb.html`.** Against the Crossan/Ehrman "left to rot / eaten by dogs" claim: crucified felons **were** buried in Palestine (**Josephus, *Jewish War* 4.310–317**), plus the Jehohanan bones — and **Crossan himself concedes** Jehohanan shows "a crucified person could receive honorable burial in the family tomb" (*Who Killed Jesus?*, 168). Check whether burial.html/emptytomb.html already carry this Crossan concession; if not, a clean P3. **OPEN.**
