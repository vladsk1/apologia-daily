## Argument map (abstract-level; [inf] = reconstructed from abstract + adjacent McGrew papers, not read)
- Abstract (verbatim fragments): "Testimonial evidence that is particularly helpful to confirmation
  combines agreement on some content with variation of detail." / "The formal analysis uses recent work
  in probability theory surrounding the concepts of dependence, independence, and varied evidence."
- [inf] Distinguishes **coherence OF reports** (mutual fit of contents — where impossibility results
  show coherence isn't truth-conducive in general) from **coherence FOR an hypothesis** (how a body of
  evidence actually raises P(H)); relocates the evidential value to joint support for H.
- [inf] **Undesigned coincidences** = the paradigm: independent testimonies that interlock while
  varying in incidental, unplanned detail — improbable under dependence (collusion/copying/legend),
  expected if the witnesses independently report a real shared event.
- Apparatus: Bayes factors / likelihood ratios; formal dependence-vs-independence conditions — a
  thoroughly **probabilistic** paper (see non-rec).
