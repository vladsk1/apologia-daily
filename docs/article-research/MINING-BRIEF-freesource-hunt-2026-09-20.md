## P2 — strong free improvements

5. **CBGM** via Peter Gurry / Text & Canon Institute (FREE) + the free SBL Press PDF of Wasserman & Gurry, *A New Approach to Textual Criticism* (2017). → `manuscript.html`. The one real gap: the essay cites the Alands/Metzger but never the *modern* method (Coherence-Based Genealogical Method / ECM) that actually produces today's text; supplies a 99.1%-agreement fidelity datum. ⚠ Measures transmission fidelity, not truth of content. textandcanon.org
6. **Lawrence Mykytiuk — Purdue institutional-repository PDFs (FREE).** → `archaeology.html`. Absent from the essay; supplies a *systematic, confidence-graded* corpus of archaeologically-confirmed biblical persons (raises "here are some hits" to a disciplined protocol). ⚠ Confirms *persons/setting*, never events/theology (the essay's "accurate setting ≠ verified event" firewall governs). docs.lib.purdue.edu/…/Mykytiuk_53_PEOPLE.pdf
7. **James F. McGrath — his own Butler Digital Commons papers (FREE):** "Monotheism"; "How Jesus Became God: One Scholar's View." → `jesus_as_god_nt.html`, `shema.html`, `jesus_claims.html`. A *more careful* unitarian foil than Ehrman (agency/monotheism via John 17:3) — raises the steelman to its best form. ⚠ Steelman only, contested; answer from the divine-identity data (`hands.html` *shaliach* material already answers his agency reading).
8. **Luke A. Barnes, "A Reasonable Little Question: A Formulation of the Fine-Tuning Argument," *Ergo* 6.42 (2019) — FREE OA.** → `finetuning.html`. Distinct from the 2012 PASA (cited) and 2018 arXiv (backlog); one paper pre-empts six objections the essay handles piecemeal. ⚠ DATA conceded, DESIGN contested — Barnes is the *theist* of the Lewis–Barnes pair, don't invert. philarchive.org/archive/BARARL-3
9. **Wes Morriston, "God and the Ontological Foundation of Morality," *Religious Studies* 48.1 (2012) — FREE (author).** → `moral.html`. The strongest current attack on Craig's *grounding* premise (the essay cites Morriston's DCT paper but not this). ⚠ Duties need a ground — never "atheists can't be moral"; Morriston grants realism, attacks only the grounding inference. spot.colorado.edu/~morristo/DoesGodGround.pdf
10. **Marijn van Putten, "'The Grace of God' as evidence for a written Uthmanic archetype," *BSOAS* 82.2 (2019) — FREE OA (Leiden).** → `islam-preservation.html`. Top-tier, recent; *grounds the essay's own bounded verdict* — a single early written archetype (vs Wansbrough) that descended by ordinary copying with its own idiosyncrasies. ⚠ Land the explicit verdict (perfect-preservation slogan is false, stated plainly); it's about *rasm*, so it doesn't touch the retired "Word of God" bridge. scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/handle/1887/79373
11. **Craig, "…A Rejoinder" + "Taking Tense Seriously" (reasonablefaith.org, FREE) + Malpass & Morriston, "Endless and Infinite," *Phil. Quarterly* (2020, PhilPapers).** → `kalam.html`. 12. **McGrew & McGrew, "The Argument from Miracles" (author PDF, FREE).** → `minimalfacts.html`. Use ONLY the *qualitative independence/cumulative* structure (the women / disciples / Paul as causally independent lines). ⚠⚠ **NO numbers/odds** — it reaches a huge Bayesian posterior and the site retired Stoner-style pseudo-math; cite their posted correction (general→high reliability). lydiamcgrew.com/…/Resurrectionarticlesinglefile.pdf
