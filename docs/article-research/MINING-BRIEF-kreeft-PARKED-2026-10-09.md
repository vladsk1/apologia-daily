## Cautions (binding when this is picked up)
- **Free to read ≠ free to copy.** Copyrighted; some essays are book excerpts. Use as LEADS
  only (`docs/article-research/README.md`): map the argument, chase the primaries, write in our
  own words, full gate. Read manually; don't scrape; store nothing copyrighted in the repo.
- **Catholic distinctives** (sacraments, Mary, papacy, purgatory) — denominational-neutrality
  rule: use only the common faith.
- **Islam — do NOT use.** *Ecumenical Jihad* (Ignatius 1996) proposes a Christian–Jewish–Muslim
  alliance against secularism; conflicts with the FALSE-COMMON-GROUND rule.
- **Popular-level, sometimes overstated.** A 21-part secular critique (Secular Frontier,
  infidels.org, 2017–18) argues several of the "twenty arguments" are not arguments for God.
  Pair every lead with stronger scholarship and the critics (same discipline as Strobel).
- **Pascal's Wager** is a reason to seek God, not evidence for him — if added, say so plainly.
- Step Zero applies: read the paired essays in full before mining; six-verdict cross-check.
