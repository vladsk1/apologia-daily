## Secondary
- **compassion:**
  - Walton & Swithinbank (eds.), *Poverty in the Early Church and Today* (OAPEN 20.500.12657/23772; licence unconfirmed).
  - Adam J. Davis, *The Medieval Economy of Salvation* (OAPEN 20.500.12657/74765; CC BY-NC-ND). Its mixed-motives framing raises neutrality issues.
  - Gregory of Nazianzus, *Oration* 43 (Schaff NPNF, public domain; HOST). The primary source for Basil's hospital.
- **abolition:** Patrick, *Letter to Coroticus* (White 1918 translation, public domain). It concerns baptised captives, so don't present it as general abolitionism.

- **manuscript:** Montoro, *Tyndale Bulletin* 76 (2025). Minor corroboration only.
