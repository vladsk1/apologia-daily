## Argument map (Carson's essay, as a map of leads)

Carson's piece is a **methodological "personal progress report,"** not a new data dump. Its spine:

1. **The Dodd thesis it reviews.** C. H. Dodd, *Historical Tradition in the Fourth Gospel* (Cambridge UP,
   1963) — building on **P. Gardner-Smith, *Saint John and the Synoptic Gospels* (Cambridge UP, 1938)** —
   argued that John is **literarily independent** of the Synoptics and so rests on its own ancient oral
   tradition, much of it as old as the Synoptic tradition. (Dodd's companion volume: *The Interpretation of
   the Fourth Gospel*, 1953.) → **the primary to know is Dodd's HTFG itself**, not Carson.
2. **Carson's key reversal — he does NOT defend John's independence.** Carson reports himself a "cautious
   convert" to **C. K. Barrett**'s position (*The Gospel According to St John*, 2nd ed., 1978) that John
   **did know Mark** (and probably Luke), and judges the full-independence consensus to be weakening. So the
   conservative heavyweight here *leans toward John's literary dependence on Mark* — the opposite of what a
   naïve miner might expect. (This is the hinge for hazard-flag 1.)
3. **Carson is also critical of Dodd's method** — he judges HTFG methodologically over-restrictive (the
   style of John is so uniform that isolating *ipsissima verba* snippets is dubious; a wholistic approach is
   better). The lead here is **the critique of the authenticity criteria**, which connects to our own
   "Turn Against the Criteria" material (see verdict ③-candidate, declined).
4. **Positive methodological reflections** (the citable *shape*, sourced to their own primaries):
   - **Presuppositions govern results** — naturalistic premises (he engages **D. E. Nineham**) rule out the
     miraculous *a priori*; that is ideology, not neutral method. → already our miracles cluster's point.
   - **The history/theology barrier is false** — a committed, even biased witness can still report accurately
     (bias ≠ falsity). → already implicit in our eyewitness treatment; Carson's own illustrations are his
     copyrighted prose and are **not** portable.
   - **Verifiable accuracies create a presumption of reliability** — where John is testable (topography,
     custom) he is strikingly accurate, so "why judged suspect where not testable?" → the one lead worth
     weighing as an improvement; declined, see ③-candidate.
   - **Early high Christology** — he cites the convergence of **C. F. D. Moule** (*The Origin of Christology*,
     1977), **I. H. Marshall** (*The Origins of New Testament Christology*, 1976), **M. Hengel** (*The Son of
     God*, ET 1976), **R. N. Longenecker** that a high Christology appeared remarkably early. → saturated in
     our deity-of-Christ cluster.
   - **Authorship** — he restates **B. F. Westcott**'s classic concentric-circles argument (the author was a
     Jew → a Palestinian Jew → an eyewitness → an apostle → John son of Zebedee) and opts for **apostolic
     authorship**, while conceding that of recent major writers "none save **Leon Morris**" still defends it.
     (This is the hinge for hazard-flag 2.) Other leads in this section: **J. A. T. Robinson**, *Redating the
     New Testament* (1976); **Morna Hooker**, "On Using the Wrong Tool" (*Theology* 75, 1972), on the misuse
     of the dissimilarity criterion; **J. L. Martyn**'s two-level-drama reading of John 9 (*History and
     Theology in the Fourth Gospel*, 1968), which Carson critiques.

## Six-verdict cross-check

- **① Corroboration — dominant.** Every substantive move in Carson is already carried by our essays, and in
  two places *more carefully*:
  - John–Synoptic independence: `multiatt.html` treats it as **debated**, hedges it in-sentence, and places
    the Mark–John pair **last** ("where John is independent of the Synoptics"). Carson's judgement that the
    independence thesis is weakening *confirms* that caution. Recorded as corroboration; no row.
  - Presupposition/naturalism-rules-out-miracle: saturated by the mined miracles cluster
    (`craig-problem-of-miracles`, `licona-vanderwatt-historians-and-miracles-2009`, `sep-miracles-mcgrew`,
    `mcgrew-argument-from-miracles-blackwell-2009`) and `library/miracles.html`.
  - Early high Christology (Moule/Marshall/Hengel/Longenecker): saturated by the deity cluster
    (`library/hands.html`, `jesus_as_god_nt.html`, `earlycreed.html`, `titles.html`, `phil2.html`) and the
    `hurtado-binitarian-devotion-pattern` note.
  - Bias ≠ falsity / committed witnesses can tell the truth: substance already in `eyewitnesses.html`
    (embarrassment + living hostile witnesses); Carson's vivid analogies are his own copyrighted prose and
    cannot be ported anyway.
- **② Error in ours — none.** Nothing in Carson contradicts a claim any of the three essays asserts.
- **③ Weaker in ours — none that warrants a row.** The lone candidate is Carson's **"verifiable accuracies →
  presumption of reliability"** move applied to John, which our essays make generally but not as a named
  John-specific argument. **Declined as a row**, for four reasons: (a) it is a *contested* inference —
  accuracy on incidental topography does not entail accuracy on miracle/theological claims, the exact point
  critics press, so it would need the "accuracy ≠ truth" firewall we already apply elsewhere; (b) a 1981
  methodological essay is a weak authority — the right primary for a John-reliability treatment is
  **Craig Blomberg, *The Historical Reliability of John's Gospel* (IVP, 2001)** or Köstenberger, not Carson;
  (c) our essays are deliberately Synoptic-and-general, not John-focused, so this is really a *new-essay*
  decision, not a wording tweak; (d) importing it would re-open dual-consensus-tier essays for a non-defect
  (rule 9 / "nine rounds is a failure"). Logged here only.
- **④ Missing from ours — none that warrants a row.** A dedicated **John-historicity essay** is genuinely
  absent site-wide, but that is a deliberate scope gap, not a Carson gap, and Carson is not its best source
  (see ③). Logged as a non-recommendation (⑥), not a row.
- **⑤ Hazard — ours is better / stay cautious. TWO, and they are the point of this mine:**
  1. **Do NOT cite Dodd/Carson to strengthen the Mark–John "independent attestation" claim.** `multiatt.html`
     deliberately hedges John's independence and ranks Mark–John last. Carson — the conservative heavyweight —
     is a "cautious convert" to Barrett's view that **John knew Mark**, and judges the independence consensus
     to be weakening. So the Dodd thesis a future session might reach for to *firm up* that card is, in
     Carson's own judgement, on the way out. Our hedge is correct and current. Keep it hedged; never upgrade it.
  2. **Do NOT assert apostolic (John son of Zebedee) authorship on `eyewitnesses.html`.** The essay
     deliberately **brackets** authorship (apostle John / John the Elder / an idealized witness — "this essay
     does not settle the question, and its argument does not depend on settling it"), and that bracketing is
     dual-consensus certified. Carson opts for apostolic authorship via Westcott, but concedes that of recent
     major writers "none save Leon Morris" still does — i.e. it is a minority scholarly position. Importing
     Westcott's argument would push the essay to a *more contested* claim than it currently holds, against its
     own certified scope. Keep the bracketing; Westcott/Carson are not a reason to drop it.
- **⑥ Non-recommendation — logged with reason.** A dedicated John's-Gospel-historicity essay would be a
  reasonable future addition, but it is an **owner-level new-essay decision**, should be built from Blomberg /
  Köstenberger primaries (not this methodological essay), and must carry the same "reliable-where-testable ≠
  true-everywhere" firewall our other reliability essays use. Deliberately not opened as a row here.

## Bottom line — NO backlog row

A clean "no new rows" mine, and a genuinely useful one despite that. The owner's instinct was right that Carson
is the conservative heavyweight on John's historical tradition — but reading him against our mature Jesus
cluster shows he **confirms our existing caution** rather than handing us a stronger card: on John's
independence he actually leans the *other* way (he thinks John knew Mark), and on authorship he holds a minority
position our essay deliberately avoids. The value banked is **two hazard flags** that keep a future session from
"upgrading" `multiatt.html`'s independence hedge or `eyewitnesses.html`'s authorship bracket backwards. One
discretionary, deliberately-declined lead: a John-specific "reliable-where-testable" essay — a new-essay,
owner-level call best built from Blomberg/Köstenberger, not from this 1981 methodological piece.
