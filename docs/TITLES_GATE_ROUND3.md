## Why this file exists — read this before fixing anything

Three lessons came out of two full rounds, and they are the reason the round-3 list below is
mostly *quotations of other people's wording* rather than instructions to write something.

**1. PORT-DON'T-AUTHOR held without a single exception, across two rounds.** Every sentence
ported verbatim from certified text survived both rounds and several were marked "preserve
verbatim." Every sentence authored in the session generated the next round's defect. Five for
five in round 1; three for three in round 2.

**2. ⭐ NEW, AND IT REFINES THE RULE: a gate's *suggested* rewrite is NOT ported wording.**
It is un-gated content and it broke exactly like authored prose.
- `apologia-neutrality` supplied the **rivalry argument** in round 1. `apologia-argument` then
  demolished it in round 2 on two grounds: it is a **non-sequitur** (the critic's case is a
  *disjunction* — "corporate, or angelic, either way not a divine individual" — and mutually
  exclusive defeaters do not weaken a disjunction), and its premise is **factually false of
  Collins**, who holds Michael receives the kingdom on behalf of the host *and* of Israel —
  which the essay's own previous paragraph states.
- `apologia-orthodoxy` drafted the **`orthonote` box** in round 1 and **overturned its own
  draft** in round 2: "Scripture never does" (of the Jesus/Michael identification) is contested
  by Calvin on Daniel 12:1, Matthew Henry, Gill and Poole on Jude 9, all reading Michael there
  as the pre-incarnate Son. It also guards the wrong perimeter — the Watchtower error is
  **creature/uncreated**, not the name "Michael."

**So: only text already carrying a `content-review` stamp counts as ported.** Everything else,
including a gate's own draft, is new content and must be re-gated.

**3. ⚠ A PORTED PREDICATE DETACHED FROM ITS QUALIFIER IS NOT A PORT.** The round-2 BREAK found
by *all three* doctrinal gates independently: I ported `ev-s3.html:916`'s "not a higher rung on
the ladder of creatures, but the Creator's own side of the line" and attached it to **the seat
alone**. In ev-s3 it attaches to **"That combination"** (seat + clouds), and ev-s3 says in terms
*"so the seat alone is not the point."* This essay's own line 141 says the same thing. So the
fix contradicted its own page one paragraph later — and that plank is the only surviving answer
to the angelic reading. **Port the qualifier with the predicate, or do not port.**
