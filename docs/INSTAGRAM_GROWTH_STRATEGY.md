### What it says, in order of severity

**① The videos are 1:16–2:12 long and earn 4–10 seconds of attention. This is the whole ballgame.**

~7% watch-through. Short-form stalls below roughly 20% and gets pushed above roughly 50%. You are

**② The retention graph is a cliff at 0:00–0:03, then dead flat.**

Look at the shape you sent: 100% → under 10% within about three seconds, then a flat line all the
way to the end. That is diagnostically specific and it is *not* "people got bored halfway."

- The flat tail means **the ~5–7% who make it past second 3 stay to the end.** The arguments are

- The cliff means **the opening frame is losing 90%+ before anyone knows what the video is.**

The mechanical cause is visible in the thumbnails you sent: the opening card is a **static block of
~12 words that must be read**. In a feed, a viewer decides in under a second, and reading is not a
decision — it is work. A static paragraph that fades in reads as a screenshot and gets flicked away
before the first clause lands.

**③ Zero saves. Zero comments. Two shares. Across 713 views.**

Saves and shares are the signals that expand reach in 2026 — they outrank likes, and DM-shares
reportedly weight 3–5× higher. You are at essentially zero on all of them. Nothing currently posted
gives anyone a reason to *keep* it or *send* it.

**④ Zero profile taps. Not "low" — zero, on all five reels.**

This kills my first draft's claim that the profile is a leak. Nobody is reaching the profile at
all. Bio copy, Highlights and the `/links` page are all real work, but they are **downstream of a
blocked pipe** and will do nothing until ① and ② are fixed. Reordered accordingly.

**⑤ The good news, and it is genuinely good: distribution is not the problem.**

76–99% of views are **non-followers**, sourced **79.7% from the Reels tab and 15.3% from Explore**.
Instagram is actively putting this in front of strangers with a 19-follower account. You do not
have a reach problem — you have a **conversion-at-every-stage** problem. That is much better news,
because reach is the thing you cannot buy your way out of and conversion is entirely under your
control.

**⑥ The one bright spot points the way.** "He was asked one question. His answer got him a death
sentence" — **10s avg watch, 11.9% watch-through, roughly double every other reel.** It is the only
one whose opening is a *curiosity gap* rather than an *assertion*. It names a thing and refuses to
say what it is. That is the pattern to industrialise.

> **One sentence:** the arguments are good, the distribution is working, and the videos are three
> times too long with an opening that asks strangers to read a paragraph. Fix the first three
> seconds and the runtime, and everything else becomes worth doing.

## 2. The strategy

**Position:** not "an apologetics page." The differentiator is already built and unused — **this is
the account that steelmans the other side before answering it, and shows its work.** 132 verified
sources, 85 cited deep dives, a named five-stage review standard, 13+ Islam essays no account this
size has. "We fact-check ourselves harder than our critics do" is a hook nobody else here can run.

### Change 1 — Cut every reel to 20–30 seconds. Do this before anything else.

Nothing else on this page matters if a stranger is asked for two minutes. At 20–30s the *same*
8-second average watch becomes 27–40% watch-through, which is the difference between "stalled" and
"pushed." **You can triple your watch-through this week without improving the content at all.**

Two ways, use both:

- **Trim the specs at source.** A 20–30s reel is **5–6 scenes at 3.5–4.5s**, not 8–12. Cut to: hook
  → the objection → the single strongest fact → the turn → the payoff. Every spec's extra scenes
  are the *second* and *third* supporting arguments — genuinely good material that belongs in the
  essay, not the reel. **The reel's job is to earn the click, not to win the argument.**
- **Start with the 5 already under 35s:** `can-we-trust-the-new-testament-manuscripts` (31s) ·
  `did-jesus-claim-to-be-god` (32s) · `can-science-explain-the-origin-of-life` (32s) ·
  `isnt-the-trinity-a-contradiction` (32s) · `what-is-the-minimal-facts-argument` (33s). Post these
  first while the rest are being trimmed — it is a free, immediate A/B against the 1:16–2:12 set.

⚠ Trimming is **re-compression of gated content**. A shortened reel is a new doctrinal artifact —
it must clear argument + orthodoxy again (+ neutrality for deity/Trinity/Islam). Cutting the
qualifying scene is exactly how an honest argument becomes an overstated one, and it is the failure
mode the 2026-07-28 pocket-card sweep exists to catch.

### Change 2 — Rebuild the first three seconds. (Faceless version.)

You are not going on camera, so the hook has to be carried by **motion, brevity and gap**. All
three are available.

| Rule | Now | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Words on the opening frame** | ~12, static | **≤5**, animated in word-by-word |
| **What the opening does** | Asserts a conclusion | **Opens a gap** — name a thing, withhold what it is |
| **Motion at 0:00** | Fade-in, then still | Kinetic type, a hard push-in, or a cut on beat 1 |
| **Audio at 0:00** | Silent | First spoken word lands inside 0.5s |

Concrete rewrites of your own five:

- "There is one seat in the universe that belongs to God alone" (4s watch) → **"One seat. In the
  whole universe."** *(then reveal)*
- "The earliest Gospel we have has Jesus charged with blasphemy. Twice." (8s) → **"Twice."**
  *(beat)* **"He was charged with blasphemy twice."**
- "Introducing the Evidence Library" (5s) → never lead with a product name. **"2,000 years. Same
  objection."**

And keep doing what the 11.9% reel did: **"He was asked one question…"** is the template. Name the
consequence, withhold the question.

### Change 3 — Add a voice. (Now a supporting fix, not the headline.)

`gen_reel.py` renders **silent by design** and writes a `.voiceover.txt` + synced `.srt` for exactly
this pass; **55 of 56 specs already carry a written voiceover script** that nothing is using. A
silent video gives a viewer nothing to hold onto in the first second, when they are deciding.

Since you are not on camera: **your own voice on the audio track is still the single strongest
trust signal available to a faceless account** — it makes it a person, not a content farm, without
you ever appearing. If you would rather not, a good TTS (ElevenLabs / CapCut) is acceptable and
still beats silence. Either way, put a quiet trending sound underneath — a discovery signal, not
content.

### Change 4 — Manufacture saves and shares. You have zero.

Views you cannot control; saves you can design for. **Saves come from utility, shares come from
"this is exactly what my friend said to me."** Neither comes from a well-argued monologue.

- **Carousels** are the save format, and ⚠ **`SOCIAL_GROWTH_PLAN.md` is wrong that they need new
  tooling** — `tools/reel/gen_card.py` already renders N branded cards from one spec at
  `--aspect portrait` (1080×1350, the correct size). Gating is the only cost.
- **End every reel with a save/share line**, not a URL. "Save this for the next time someone asks
  you." The site's own **"send this to a skeptic"** framing is already written and is the strongest
  lever available.
- **Ask a question in the caption.** Zero comments across 713 views is partly because nothing ever
  invites one.

### Change 5 — Series, then profile. (Deliberately last.)

Numbering posts (*Objection #14*) converts "nice fact" into "I'm mid-way through something," and
the bio/Highlights/`/links` work makes a profile tap convert. **Both are real and both are wasted
until ①–② land** — at 0 profile taps there is nobody to convert. Do them in week 3, not week 1.

Two exceptions worth doing now because they cost nothing: **unfollow down to <20** (55:19 the wrong
way reads as a bot) and **get past 100 followers by hand** — church, small group, family. Under
100, the number itself is an objection.

### Change 6 — Post the same file to TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Three extra minutes per post. At 19 followers Instagram is the *hardest* of the three to grow.
**Shorts compounds with work already done:** it is search-indexed and the site has 102 answer pages
targeting the same questions — title each Short with the question **verbatim** from the matching
`/answers/` page. It is the only channel where a month-6 upload still earns.

## 4. The daily plan

**40–60 min/day + one 2-hour Saturday production block.** Built for a solo operator, deliberately
survivable. The owner is doing the engagement; that is the highest-leverage 20 minutes in the plan.

### The repeating week

| Day | Post (one, same hour daily) | Stories | Engagement — 20 min |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Reel — *Objection #N* (20–30s) | Poll: "Have you been asked this?" | 10 substantive comments on peer accounts (5–50K) |
| **Tue** | Carousel — *Even the skeptics grant…* | Reshare Mon's reel + one added line | 10 comments; reply to all comments on Mon–Tue |
| **Wed** | Reel — *The verse they use on you* | A source page / a citation being checked | 10 comments |
| **Thu** | Carousel — question breakdown | Poll: "Which do we answer next?" | 10 comments; DM-reply every story replier |
| **Fri** | Reel — *Objection #N+1* | Reshare the week's best | 10 comments |
| **Sat** | *(no post)* | 1 reshare | **2h production block** + reply to every comment of the week |
| **Sun** | Pastoral post, gentler register | "For anyone having a hard week" | Rest |

**Each posting day (~25 min):**

1. **Post at the same hour** — pick 9am *or* 7pm and hold it 30 days.
2. **Cross-post the identical file natively** to TikTok + YouTube Shorts within 10 min. Never a link,
   never a watermarked re-share. Shorts title = the question **verbatim** from the answer page.
3. **Caption:** hook restated → one-line promise → **a question** (you have 0 comments; ask for one)
   → **"Save this for the next time someone asks you."** URL last or not at all.
4. **Hashtags: a fixed pool of 4–5, unchanged for 30 days** — rotating them stops the algorithm ever
   establishing topical authority. `#apologetics #christianity #jesus #faith` + one topical.
5. **First 60 minutes: reply to every comment.** Early comment velocity is a direct ranking input.
6. **Log it.** One row per post: length · views · **avg watch** · **watch-through** · skip rate ·
   saves · shares · profile taps. Watch-through is the number you are steering by, not views.

**Saturday block (2h):**

| Min | Task |
|---|---|
| 0–30 | **Trim next week's 3 specs to 5–6 scenes / 20–30s.** Queue them for the gate |
| 30–50 | Render: `gen_reel.py` ×3 · `gen_card.py --aspect portrait` ×2 |
| 50–85 | Voiceover pass (read the `.voiceover.txt`), quiet trending sound, export watermark-free |
| 85–100 | Rebuild the opening 3s of each: ≤5 words, animated in, gap not assertion |
| 100–120 | Captions, schedule all five, review last week's log, pick next week's |

**Never post from an empty queue.** A week produced ahead is the difference between this running six
months and stopping in week 3.

## 5. Targets — steer by watch-through, not followers

| | Now | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Watch-through** ⭐ | **~7%** | **>25%** | >35% | >45% |
| Skip rate | 73% | <60% | <50% | <45% |
| Saves per reel | **0** | >3 | >10 | >25 |
| Profile taps per reel | **0** | >5 | >20 | >50 |
| Views per reel | ~140 | 300–800 | 1,000–2,500 | 2,500–6,000 |
| Followers | 19 | 100–250 | 350–700 | 900–1,800 |

**Watch-through is the only number that matters in month 1.** It moves first, it is fully under your
control, and everything else follows it. Followers move last and are the worst thing to steer by.

**If day-30 watch-through is still under 15%:** the length cut happened but the hook rebuild didn't.
Post three reels where the opening frame is **three words**, and compare. If that also fails, the
remaining untried lever is a face — worth revisiting the camera decision once, on evidence.

**If watch-through clears 25% but profile taps stay near 0:** now the profile *is* the leak, and
Change 5 becomes the priority. Do it properly then.

**Do not**, in the first 90 days: boost posts, pay creators, pitch Collabs above 100K, or run Meta
ads (blocked independently — `privacy.html:202` promises no advertising cookies, and the CAC
arithmetic in `META_ADS_PLAN.md` does not work).
