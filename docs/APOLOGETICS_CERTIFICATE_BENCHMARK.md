## 2. What the best programs have in common

Price and accreditation aside, the strongest programs share seven traits:

1. **Named, credentialed faculty.** Biola sells Craig, Moreland and McDowell. OCCA sells Lennox and
   McGrath. **This is the trait we most lack.**
2. **The same three core pillars**, in almost every program:
   (a) God exists (natural theology);
   (b) Jesus is historical and rose from the dead (Gospels, resurrection);
   (c) Scripture is reliable.
   Biola's three courses *are* these three pillars.
3. **A method or systems course.** SES and HCU both require one: classical, evidential,
   presuppositional, cumulative-case and Reformed-epistemology approaches.
4. **Worldview and rival-religion specialisms.** SES has a whole Islamic Studies certificate. HCU
   has cultural apologetics.
5. **A communication/practice component.** HCU has an "Apologetics Communication" certificate. OCCA
   runs preaching workshops and outreach. CIA is mostly speaking and Q&A. STR teaches "wisdom" (tactics)
   and "character".
6. **Assessment that proves the learning.** This ranges from Biola's per-lecture quizzes up to
   STR's test-out exam and CIA's live teaching.
7. **Some human contact.** Biola's residential week, OCCA's live seminars and CIA's intensive all
   provide it. Fully self-paced programs (Biola LEARN, Magis) are the cheap end of the market.

Also worth noting:
- **Tiering works.** STR has Silver and Gold levels. SBTS has standard and advanced certificates.
  Biola lets its certificate count toward the MA. Learners like a ladder.
- **Institutional recognition is a strong hook.** Magis's catechist CE hours make a $149
  certificate something a parish will pay for.

## 3. Where we stand against them

**We already have more curriculum-grade content than any Tier-A certificate.** Counted 2026-09-23:
- **94** `library/*.html` deep-dive essays (fully footnoted)
- **75** `ev-m-*` mastery pages, each with nine layers, including an AI-graded *Explain It Back*
- **8** Evidence Library tabs
- **111** answer pages
- **31** public-domain source files

On top of that we have flashcards, pocket cards, the Memory Palace, the Debate Arena, study plans,
study groups, reading clubs, the Conversation Journal, and a published five-stage review standard,
"Checked Before Published".

Biola's certificate is about **24 lectures on three topics**. Our library covers those three pillars
plus the Trinity, deity of Christ, Islam, JW, Mormonism, evil and church history, in more depth.

**What we lack is not content. It is the certificate machinery around the content, plus four
specific gaps:**

| Gap | Who has it | Our status |
|---|---|---|
| **A structured, sequenced curriculum with a start and an end** | All of them | We have study plans and a Beginner's Path, but **no defined course a learner can complete** |
| **Summative assessment** | Biola quizzes, STR exam, CIA live teaching | Per-page mastery dials and quizzes, but **no final exam and no capstone** |
| **A credential** | All of them | **None.** No certificate, no verification page, nothing to share |
| **An apologetic-method module** | SES, HCU | **Absent.** No page teaches the approaches themselves |
| **A history of apologetics** | SES, HCU, OCCA | Partly covered by ev-s8 and the `/sources` Fathers, but **no module** |
| **Communication / tactics / pastoral practice** | HCU, STR, OCCA, CIA | The Debate Arena gives us practice, but **there is no taught module** |
| **Named human faculty / pastoral sign-off** | All of them | 🔴 **The main credibility gap.** Our reviewers are AI under human supervision, and `docs/STATEMENT_OF_FAITH.md` still logs the pastoral reviewer as `_pending_` |

## 4. Recommendation — what to build

### 4.1 Principle: **assemble, don't author**

The strongest move comes straight from this repo's own experience. **Every ported sentence has
survived the gates, and nearly every authored sentence has produced the next defect.** A certificate
is mostly a **sequence** of material, plus assessment and a credential. So most of it can be built by
sequencing already-certified essays and mastery pages, and needs very little new doctrinal prose.
New writing is needed only for the three missing modules (§4.3), and those go through the full
pipeline.

### 4.2 Proposed structure — a three-level ladder

The levels model STR's Silver/Gold and SBTS's standard/advanced. All names are placeholders.

**Level 1 — Foundations** *(matches Biola's certificate, and goes further)*
- Module 1 **Does God exist?**: kalam, fine-tuning, moral argument, plus the deism fence (the
  argument reaches a Mind, not yet the Triune God).
- Module 2 **Can we trust the Bible?**: manuscripts, canon, archaeology. Preservation is not
  truth.
- Module 3 **Did Jesus rise?**: early creed, minimal facts, empty tomb, disciples' belief.
- Module 4 **How to talk about it**: new, see §4.3.
- Each module is 4–6 existing mastery pages in order, with a module test at the end.

**Level 2 — Practitioner**
- The deity of Christ and the Trinity (ev-s3, ev-s6). Dual-consensus tier throughout.
- Rival worldviews: Islam, JW, Mormonism, atheism. The EXPLICIT-VERDICT and FALSE-COMMON-GROUND
  rules apply.
- Suffering and evil. Concede the mystery; no tidy theodicy.
- Church in history (ev-s8).
- **Apologetic method**: new, see §4.3.
- **Practicum**: a set of graded Debate Arena rounds against named personas.

**Level 3 — Advanced / Teacher**
- History of apologetics (new).
- A **capstone**: a written case on a chosen topic, plus an oral defence against the Debate Arena.
- **Teach-back**: run a four-week study-group plan with at least three people. This is our version
  of CIA's "learn to teach it".
- Human review of the capstone once a reviewer exists (§4.5).

### 4.3 The three genuinely new modules

Each of these is **new authored content**, so each needs the full pipeline, with a
citations → argument → orthodoxy gate at minimum.

1. **Apologetic method.** Classical, evidential, cumulative-case, presuppositional, Reformed
   epistemology. ⚠ **This is a denominational-neutrality hazard**, because presuppositionalism vs
   evidentialism is an intra-Protestant fight. Present each approach in its own strongest terms and
   **do not crown a winner.** Run `apologia-neutrality`.
2. **History of apologetics.** Justin, Origen *Against Celsus*, Augustine, Aquinas, Pascal, Paley,
   Newman, Lewis. **Ground it in `/sources`, using verified PD passages only.** Include Catholic and
   Orthodox figures on purpose; that is what "common Christian faith" requires.
3. **Communication and pastoral practice.** Questions over assertions, gentleness and respect
   (1 Peter 3:15), when to stop arguing, and **when the right answer is a referral, not an
   argument.** Port the PASTORAL CARE exception from `api/ask.js` into teaching. Tactics must be
   **in our own words**. Koukl's *Tactics* may be used as a book-research lead only; never
   reproduce it.

### 4.4 Assessment and credential — the machinery

- **Module test**: a randomised draw from a question bank. ⚠ **Two hard prerequisites from this
  repo's own history:**
  - (a) **Every question and every graded answer is gated content.** `daily-mix.html` once graded a
    retired claim as the *correct answer*. Nothing that marks a reader right or wrong ships ungated.
  - (b) **Fix the answer-length tell first.** In `speed-round.html` the correct answer is
    longest-or-tied in **40 of 60** items, against a 25% baseline. A certificate exam with that
    tell can be passed without learning anything. Per the backlog, the fix must not come from
    padding the distractors with ungated doctrinal prose.
- **Pass rule for a module**: mastery dial complete, **Explain It Back ≥ 7/10** on each core
  argument, and **module test ≥ 80%**.
- **Credential**: a certificate PDF plus a **public verification page** (`/verify/<id>`: name,
  level, date, modules). Also an "Add to LinkedIn" link and a shareable card built with the brand
  card generator.
- **Language.** Call it a **"Certificate of Completion"** and state plainly that it is **not academic
  credit**. **Do not use "diploma"**: in several jurisdictions (e.g. Australia's AQF) it names a
  regulated qualification. The certificate page should link `editorial-standards.html` and say
  honestly how the material is reviewed.
- **Engineering.** This needs a Supabase `certificates` table with RLS (the user can read their
  own; the verify page reads a public projection), plus server-side scoring so a result cannot be
  forged from the client. Reuse the `progress-sync.js` / `user_progress` table that went live on
  2026-09-08. **`apologia-engineer` must review it**, and it touches auth and paywall code.

### 4.5 Close the faculty gap honestly

This is the one area where copying the universities would mean overstating ourselves. Options, in
order of preference:
1. **Recruit a small advisory board**: one or two credentialed theologians or pastors, ideally
   spanning Protestant, Catholic and Orthodox. Name them on the certificate page. **This also closes
   the pastoral sign-off debt**, which has been open since the Statement of Faith was written.
   ⚠ **Do not issue a doctrinal credential before this.** A certificate says "this person
   understands orthodox doctrine", and today no human has signed off on the doctrine being taught.
2. **Interview-format guest content.** Short recorded conversations with scholars, sourced and
   gated. Our version of Biola's named lecturers.
3. Until then, say what we are: **a rigorously reviewed library with AI-assisted review under
   human supervision.** Do not borrow academic trappings.

### 4.6 Pricing and positioning

Reported benchmarks: Magis $149 · Biola $350 · OCCA £380 · SES ~$7,125 list for 15 credits.
- A natural position is **Level 1 inside Pro, or as a one-off at about the Magis price
  point**. Levels 2–3 and group or church licences sit higher. OCCA's group discounts and Magis's
  parish model are both precedents.
- ⚠ **This is blocked on the open pricing/paywall decision.** `isPro` is still hardcoded `true`,
  Stripe is not wired, and in-app purchases fall under Apple 3.1.1 (see `docs/APP_STORE.md`
  §1.5). **Do not sell a certificate before the paywall is real.**
- **Church partnership.** A catechist-hours arrangement like Magis's requires diocesan approval and
  is tied to one tradition. For a neutral product, a **church/group licence plus a leader's guide**
  (building on the study-groups feature) is the honest equivalent.
- **Long term (only if the advisory board exists):** approach **SES or HCU about credit articulation**,
  the way Biola lets its certificate count toward the MA. Speculative; not before Level 1 has
  graduates.

## 5. Sequenced roadmap

| Phase | Work | Gates / blockers |
|---|---|---|
| **0 — Decide** | Owner decisions: pricing/paywall; certificate name; whether to recruit an advisory board first | Owner |
| **1 — Level 1 as a sequence** | Define modules from existing certified pages. Build the course-progress UI on `user_progress`. **No new doctrinal prose** | `apologia-engineer` |
| **2 — Assessment** | Question bank drawn from certified pages. Fix the answer-length tell. Server-side scoring | **Every question gated** (argument + orthodoxy; + neutrality for deity/Trinity/Islam) |
| **3 — Communication module** | Author module 4 | Full pipeline + `apologia-neutrality` |
| **4 — Credential** | Certificate PDF, `/verify/<id>`, share card | Engineer review. **Blocked on advisory board / pastoral sign-off (§4.5)** |
| **5 — Level 2** | Method module (neutrality hazard), practicum rubric | Full pipeline, dual-consensus |
| **6 — Level 3** | History module (from `/sources`), capstone, teach-back, human capstone review | Needs the human reviewer |

**What can start today without any owner decision:** Phase 1, mapping the Level 1 modules onto
existing mastery pages. It is structural, uses only certified content, and is reversible.
