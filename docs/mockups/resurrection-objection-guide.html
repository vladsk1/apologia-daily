Resurrection Objection Guide

This shows how the "argument graph" would look to a reader: start from what someone said to you, and get to the answer, the evidence, the scholars and the pitfalls on one screen.

  • Answer and evidence text is copied from our already-reviewed essays (their FAQ answers and "See the evidence" panels), trimmed only so each page opens with the answer.
  • Labels, headings and "built from" lines are placeholder UI copy and have not been reviewed.
  • Tap a scholar's name to see what they grant and dispute. That is the graph at work: one scholar record, reused on every objection.
  • The buttons link to the real essays and practice pages on apologiadaily.com.

Not a live page, and not certified. Before anything like this ships, every string goes through the normal review.
