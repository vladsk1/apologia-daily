# Known traps — the mistakes this project has actually made

**Load this at the start of every `apologia-orthodoxy` and `apologia-neutrality` run.**
It is not a theory of what *could* go wrong; every entry is an error that **actually reached
a draft or production** on this site and was caught by a human or a review agent. Re-checking
these specific traps on each run is how we stop re-making them. Each entry: the trap · why it's
wrong · what to say instead.

> Maintenance: when a gate (or a human) catches a new recurring error, add it here in the same
> change, harvested from the `content-review` stamp / CLAUDE.md status entry that records it.
> Keep it curated — this is a working checklist, not a changelog.

## Doctrine — precision errors (orthodox-sounding, technically wrong)

- **Relation of origin swapped.** The **Son is begotten/generated**; the **Spirit proceeds**.
  Never say the Son "proceeds from the Father" or the Spirit is "begotten/generated." Shipped in
  the `if-jesus-is-god-why-did-he-pray` clarifier box (2026-10-31); not heresy, so the heresy scan
  passed it. Mechanically guarded now (`retired-claims.json` → `relation-of-origin-son-spirit-swap`).
- **Patripassianism by grammar.** "God/the Son was crucified" is orthodox *only* fenced: the Son
  suffered **in his assumed human nature**; the **divine nature did not die** and the **Father was
  not crucified**. A bare "the divine nature died" / "God suffered as God" is theopaschite.
- **John 5:23 inverted.** "Honouring the Son **as** the Father" compresses into a modalist reading
  ("the Son just *is* the Father"). The verse's point is that the Son is honoured *with the same
  worship due to God* — say that, don't drop "as God."
- **Aseity "granted."** Saying the Son "receives his aseity/self-existence from the Father" brushes
  the *autotheos* debate; keep "the **person** of the Son is eternally from the Father, the **deity**
  is underived" and never make it a dependence in being. (Live NOTE on `ev-s6`/`eternal_generation`.)
- **Chalcedon gap.** Deity-of-Christ content that affirms full deity but never affirms full
  **humanity** leaves a functionally monophysite Christ. Affirm both (one person, two natures).

## Doctrine — retired claims (orthodox-but-withdrawn, or overreach)

- **`pelach` "only to God."** Daniel uses *pelach* of Nebuchadnezzar's gods (Dan 3:12), so "the
  Aramaic word for worship is used only of God" is false. Don't rest the Son-of-Man case on it.
- **"Divine, worshipped figure of Daniel 7."** Retired — the figure in Daniel 7 is enthroned/served
  but the text does not straightforwardly predicate deity+worship; `titles.html` took five rounds to
  remove it. Recurs in popular deity-of-Christ writing (found in two book-research notes). Grep new
  content for it.
- **`echad` = "compound unity."** The Shema's *echad* does not lexically prove plurality-in-unity;
  `shema.html` says "honesty requires saying no." Don't run it as a proof.
- **Colwell's rule → qualitative.** Colwell (1933) is about **definiteness**. The *qualitative*
  reading of John 1:1c is **Harner/Dixon/Wallace** — and Wallace warns against the Colwell
  misattribution. Never credit the qualitative force to Colwell.
- **NWT "throughout John 1."** The NWT prints "the only-begotten **god**" (lowercase) at John 1:18,
  so "it renders anarthrous *theos* as 'God' throughout the chapter" is false; a well-read Witness
  has 1:18 ready. Name 1:6/1:12/1:13 specifically.
- **Stoner's odds / "the statistical case" for prophecy.** Retired as pseudo-mathematics; don't
  revive probability figures (incl. on the memorisation surfaces).
- **"Nicaea, 300 bishops."** Use "roughly 250 to 318" (the sources vary); "300" is rounded folklore.
- **Francis Collins for ID.** Collins founded BioLogos to *oppose* the design inference — never
  recruit him as a premise for intelligent design.
- **"Paul wrote the creed."** Paul **received** the 1 Cor 15:3–7 formula (he says so); it predates
  him. Don't say he authored it.
- **"7 manuscripts for Plato."** A discredited apologetics figure; don't use the manuscript-count
  comparison in that form.

## Rival-worldview — false common ground & tilts

- **"Same God" (Islam).** Shared words ≠ shared belief. Islam names Jesus "Messiah/Word", affirms
  the virgin birth, "honours" Jesus — while emptying each term (it honours him *by refusing him
  worship as God*). State the shared vocabulary as an on-ramp; never as shared faith/"common ground."
- **Islam lexical bridge.** Don't argue "Qur'an = eternal Word + Qur'an calls Jesus 'Word' ⇒ Jesus
  is the eternal Word" — it equivocates *kalam* (God's speech) with *kalima* (4:171, of Jesus). Run
  it **structurally** (the Mihna / uncreated-speech category), scoped to Ash'ari/Maturidi.
- **Ash'ari formula.** Render it "neither **He** nor other than He" (attribute vis-à-vis the
  essence), never "neither **God** nor other than God" (predicates near-deity of an attribute).
- **Bible↔Qur'an "same test / same standard."** Surah 4:82's no-contradiction test is the Qur'an's
  *own, stricter, self-issued* standard — don't grant a symmetry the evidence doesn't establish.

## Scholars & sources — labels and attributions

- See **`docs/scholar-labels.md`** for the full ledger. Highest-frequency: **Penrose** is
  "non-believing," not flatly "atheist" (mixed self-description); **Geraint Lewis** is the
  **multiverse advocate** and **Luke Barnes** the theist in *A Fortunate Universe* — never call
  Lewis "the sceptic" unqualified; **Mettinger** (dying-and-rising gods) is the *mythicists' own
  source* and holds a minority position he himself concedes; **Bauckham** — cite as a historian on
  historical questions only, never in a further-reading list without naming the work (universalism
  fence); **Ehrman/Allison/Stenger/Vermes/BeDuhn** have all been mislabelled here before.

## Fine-tuning — the fragile one

- **Data vs design.** The fine-tuning **data** is conceded even by non-believing physicists; that it
  points to **design** is a contested philosophical **inference**. Never "scientists agree the
  universe is designed."
- **Stenger.** Don't claim unanimity on *how narrow* the windows are — Victor Stenger disputed
  exactly that, and `finetuning.html`'s own proof-text (Barnes 2012) names him "the antagonist."
- **Hossenfelder.** Her untestability critique must carry her symmetry disclosure, or a
  methodological critic of multiverse reasoning becomes a witness *for* design.

## History

- **Julian the Apostate (AD 362).** *Post*-Constantine — NOT evidence of 2nd/3rd-century growth, and
  never list him among the illegal-period witnesses (Pliny c.112, Lucian c.165, Cornelius 251). The
  vivid "impious Galilaeans" line is from the *Letter to Arsacius*, which Van Nuffelen argues is a
  **forgery** — ground him in the undisputed *Fragment of a Letter to a Priest*.
