# Influencer / creator growth plan — Apologia Daily

_Written 2026-09-05. Supersedes the offer design in `docs/CREATOR_OUTREACH.md` (2026-07-06) and
extends it with the September realities from `docs/MARKET_RESEARCH_2026-09-03.md`. The July target
shortlist and the gift-then-ask *sequence* still stand and are not repeated here — read that file

offer is dead, and the winning role for a faceless brand is different from what July assumed.**_

---

## 0. The two facts that decide everything

1. **The product is effectively free.** 78 files hardcode `isPro = true`, there is no Stripe
   (`api/`/`lib/` carry none), and no paywall gates anything. So the July offer — *"free lifetime
   Pro + 30% first-year rev-share code"* — **has nothing to give and nothing to pay**: you cannot
   gift Pro (everyone has it) and you cannot rev-share a $0 product. **Do not send the rev-share
   pitch.** A creator who runs a "get commission" code into a free product looks foolish, and one
   who sends followers to a dead checkout burns their own trust (July's own rule).
2. **Apologia is a faceless brand, and that is fixed strategy, not a gap.** The owner decided: no
   camera, faceless-optimised (`INSTAGRAM_GROWTH_STRATEGY.md`). The market research is blunt about
   what that means: *a faceless brand cannot be the face — it can be the place the faces link to.*
   Every apologetics creator who grew in 2026 (Huff, the Knechtles, Turek) did it with a face and a
   voice. **So the influencer strategy is not "borrow their reach to sell." It is "become the
   receipts layer behind their content"** — the fact-checked source they cite, screenshot, and link.

**The reframed offer, in one line:** *We don't want a shout-out. We want to be the fact-checked
source your scripts run on — and we'll do work for you to earn it.*

## 1. The give — what a free brand can actually offer a creator (this is the whole plan)

Because there's no Pro to gift and no commission to pay, the give has to be **labour and
credibility**, not money or access. Four gives, strongest first — and the first one is a genuine
moat:

**A. A finished, fact-checked short-form video, made for them, free.** The `make-reel` skill renders
brand-styled, fully-captioned reels from certified content locally (no cost per unit). **We can hand
a creator a done reel on any topic they name** — either Apologia-branded (they repost, it links back)
or, for a real partner, *co-branded / unbranded to their look*. Almost no one in this niche can offer
"tell me your next topic and I'll send you a gated, sourced 30-second video by Friday." This is the
ownable give. ⚠ Every such reel is site content → full pipeline (argument + orthodoxy, +neutrality
for deity/Islam) before it leaves the building.

**B. A "receipts" link for their claims.** Creators make assertions on camera ("the creed dates to
within 5 years") and get torn apart in comments for not sourcing them. Offer a **per-topic deep-link**
to the certified essay + its footnotes as the pinned-comment source. Their audience gets the receipts;
we get the backlink and the qualified traffic. This is also the AI-citation play (§4).

**C. Script fact-checking / a private source library.** Offer to read a creator's draft script and
flag the claims that won't survive a knowledgeable critic (the fabricated-quote class the site was
built to catch). For emerging creators this is enormously valuable and builds a real relationship.

**D. Co-authored / credited content.** For the right person, invite them to co-write an essay or be
a named contributor — which shades into the highest-value tier below.

## 2. Three tiers of "influencer" — different people, different asks

July treated influencers as one list (social creators). There are actually three, and the most
valuable one isn't on social media.

### Tier 1 — Scholar-reviewers (HIGHEST value; doubles as the #1 growth move)
The market research names a **named credentialed reviewer** as the single highest-leverage move for
the whole platform — it's the one thing no apologetics site has, and it's the missing credibility
moat. **Recruiting 2–3 credentialed people to review essays IS influencer involvement of the most
durable kind:** a philosophy PhD, an NT/OT PhD, and a pastor — ideally from *different* traditions so
neutrality is visible. Their names on a `/review-board.html` page and in each essay's byline are
worth more than any social shout-out, and they bring their own academic networks.
- **Ask:** review 3–5 essays/month at the dual-consensus tier (Trinity/deity/Islam first).
- **Give:** a credited, public scholarly role on a serious project; co-authorship where earned.
- ⚠ Never put a reviewer's name on anything they haven't actually reviewed (stamp discipline applies
  to people exactly as to gates). This also finally closes the standing `STATEMENT_OF_FAITH.md`
  pastoral-sign-off debt.

### Tier 2 — Micro/mid apologetics creators (the July shortlist — the amplifiers)
1k–120k, evidence-first, calm tone. **This is where `CREATOR_OUTREACH.md`'s named list applies** —
Sechler, Carnahan, Torres, Girl Talk, Barnett/Red Pen, Think Christ, Caleb McManus, plus campus/RF
chapter accounts. The list is sound; only the *offer* changes (use §1 gives, not rev-share).
- **Ask:** use us for prep; mention/link when it's genuinely useful; repost a reel we make them.
- **Give:** gives A–C above.

### Tier 3 — The big faces (aspirational; audience-engagement, not partnership)
Huff (~700k), the Knechtles (~950k), Turek (~845k), Inspiring Philosophy, Ortlund. **Do not pitch a
partnership** — wrong altitude, and cold-DMing big creators reads as spam. Two real plays:
- **Engage the audience, not the creator** (July's GodLogic tactic): leave genuinely substantive,
  sourced, gracious replies in their comment sections and under the viral clips their topics spawn.
  This is the single best faceless-brand distribution tactic and it needs no one's permission.
- **The long game:** one credible long-form podcast appearance, chopped into clips, beat months of
  native posting for Huff. If the owner ever fronts the brand, a guest slot on a mid-tier apologetics
  podcast is the highest-yield single act. (Owner call; the brand is faceless by choice today.)

## 3. Discovery — filling the list (the structural finding still holds)

Web search cannot see true 1k–15k creators. Filling Tier 2 to 20–25 names needs **a human with
TikTok/IG/X access, ~2 hours** (unchanged from July, restated because it's still the blocker):
1. Mine comment/stitch/duet sections under Carnahan, Torres, Barnett, and under the big Tier-3 faces.
2. Scroll `#christianapologetics`, `#apologetics`, `#applogetics` (the common misspelling),
   `#faithandreason`, `#answeringskeptics`.
3. Follow the "suggested accounts" chain off the confirmed micro names.
4. **Campus apologetics clubs + Reasonable Faith / Ratio Christi chapters** — student-run accounts,
   tiny, perfectly aligned, near-certain yes. This overlaps the institutional distribution play in
   the market research (offer the Islam cluster as a free chapter resource).
Log each find with date + sourced follower count. **Never invent a number.**

## 4. Why this compounds — the AI-citation flywheel

Every creator link, pinned-comment receipt, and Reddit/thread citation is not just referral traffic
(which is small and one-shot). It's **third-party corroboration**, which is what gets a site cited
inside AI answers — and the research shows brands are ~6.5× more likely to be cited via third parties
than their own site. So creator involvement does double duty: a little direct traffic now, and a
durable rise in being the source ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI name later. This is the strategic reason
to pay creators in *labour* (make them reels, source their scripts) rather than chase a shout-out: the
link is the asset, and the link only sticks if the content is genuinely useful to them.

## 5. Tracking (what's already wired)

- **Per-creator URLs work today:** `signup.html:164` captures `?ref=` (and a stored `ad_ref`). Give
  each partner `apologiadaily.com/?ref=<handle>`; signups carry the code. ⚠ Confirm the captured
  `referredBy` is actually persisted server-side to the signup row before promising anyone attribution.
- **PostHog** already emits `campaign_landing` with utm/ref (`analytics.js`). Tag every creator link
  with `?utm_source=<handle>&utm_medium=creator` so the funnel is readable — **once someone actually
  reads the nine months of PostHog data**, which is still the cheapest unblocked growth task.
- A lightweight **`docs/creator-crm.md`** (or a sheet): handle · tier · size · date contacted · stage
  (engaged / gifted / replied / linked) · ref code · outcome. Keep it measured, not estimated.

## 6. Guardrails (non-negotiable — these have killed outreach ideas before)

- **Denominational neutrality.** Pitch only denominationally-neutral content to tradition-specific
  creators (e.g. a Catholic-convert creator gets neutral essays, never intra-Christian dispute
  material). Never let a partnership imply the site takes a side Catholics/Orthodox/Protestants split on.
- **1 Peter 3:15 tone = a hard filter on *who*.** Exclude combative "blunder"/debate-dunk creators
  even at high reach (GodLogic excluded in July on exactly this ground). A partner's tone becomes ours.
- **Nicene guardrail = a hard doctrinal exclude.** No LDS/JW creators as partners (Chandler Hendry
  excluded in July though format/tone fit). Their *audiences* are people to serve, not creators to ally.
- **The Islam cluster is not a paid-distribution or aggressive-partnership asset** (ministry, brand,
  and account-survival grounds, per `META_ADS_PLAN.md`). Offer it quietly as a resource; don't build
  a campaign around "answering Islam" with a combative creator.
- **No rev-share / paid-endorsement pitch until Stripe is live** (and even then, only to a warm
  reply). Today the offer is labour + credibility, full stop.
- **Every reel/carousel/essay we make for a creator is site content** → full pipeline, orthodoxy last,
  a fix pass re-opens the gate, port don't author. A co-branded video that ships an overclaim damages
  two brands at once.
- **Human/pastoral sign-off is still owed site-wide.** The scholar-reviewer tier (§2.1) is how that
  finally gets closed — don't treat the AI gates as the sign-off when recruiting real reviewers.

## 7. Sequenced 60-day plan

**Weeks 1–2 — make the brand linkable before asking anyone to link it.**
- Wire the social links the site still lacks (`twitter:site` is on 0 of 1,108 pages; no footer social
  icons beyond IG). A creator who checks us out should find a real, active presence.
- Confirm `?ref=` persists server-side; add the `utm_source=…&utm_medium=creator` convention.
- Stand up `docs/creator-crm.md`. Read the PostHog data so you can measure what outreach does.
- **Owner, in parallel:** start the Tier-1 scholar-reviewer conversations — the long pole, and the
  highest-value influencer relationship on this whole page.

**Weeks 3–5 — the give, proven on three people.**
- Pick 3 Tier-2 micro creators from the July list. For each: 2 weeks of genuine engagement (comments,
  a share), then **the gift with no ask** — *"I made you a fact-checked reel on [their topic], yours to
  use, no strings,"* plus a receipts link. Gate every asset first.
- Begin the **comment-section value tactic** under 2–3 Tier-3 faces (sourced, gracious replies).

**Weeks 6–8 — convert and widen.**
- Only creators who replied warmly get the light ask (link us / use us for prep). Track ref signups.
- Human does the 2-hour discovery pass (§3); grow the Tier-2 list to ~20 with sourced numbers.
- First scholar review lands → publish `/review-board.html` with the first named byline → *that page
  itself* becomes outreach material ("reviewed by [credentialed name]") for the next creator round.

**Standing rule:** measure, don't estimate — every follower count sourced and dated, every "it
worked" backed by a PostHog number, no invented figures (the handover discipline in `CLAUDE.md`).

   partner. Owner's call on where the line sits.

   just needs a live checkout and a warm relationship first. Until then it stays in the drawer.

   where a small amount of money (or a real title) unlocks the highest-value people.
