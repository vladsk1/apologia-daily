Jesus is God

◆

The HANDS Framework — five reasons from the Bible

Apologia Daily · apologiadaily.com

◆ The big idea

Five things that belong to God alone

The New Testament gives Jesus five things that, in the Bible, belong to God alone — the Honors, Attributes, Names, Deeds, and Seat of God.

The earliest Christians were Jews who would rather die than worship an idol. Yet they gave Jesus the honor due to God alone — and still believed they were faithful to the one God. They were not turning a man into a god. They had come to see that Jesus, the crucified Galilean they knew, is God himself — truly God, and truly man.

This study walks through all five strands of evidence, so you can see for yourself how the New Testament presents Jesus — and carry the case in your hand.

◆ Where it comes from

Not a new idea

A simple way to organise what the Bible already says. Leading scholars gather the same evidence:

HANDS

Robert Bowman & J. Ed Komoszewski, evangelical scholars, in Putting Jesus in His Place (2007).

Divine identity

Richard Bauckham: the one God is known by who he is — he alone creates, rules, bears the divine name, and is worshipped. The New Testament deliberately places Jesus inside that identity.

Early worship

Larry Hurtado: devotion to Jesus as divine appears at the earliest stage we can see — already there among the first Jewish believers, not a belief that grew up gradually in later generations.

◆ The big picture

One hand, five fingers

Each finger stands for one thing that belongs to God alone. The New Testament gives all five to Jesus.

One finger can be discussed on its own. Five fingers, all pointing the same way, are far harder to explain.

H

◆ The first finger · Honors

H
Honors

He receives the honor due to God

“Honor” here means worship, prayer, and reverence. In John 5:23 Jesus says the Father gave him all judgment “that all may honor the Son, just as they honor the Father.” The standard is not merely like God’s — it is identical to it.

“…to him who sits on the throne and to the Lamb be blessing and honor and glory.”Revelation 5:13

In the same book an angel twice refuses worship: “Worship God!” (Revelation 19:10; 22:9). The Lamb receives what the angel forbids.

The evidence

  • John 5:23Honor the Son just as they honor the Father.
  • Revelation 5:13–14Every creature worships the One on the throne and the Lamb.
  • Hebrews 1:6Even the angels: “Let all God’s angels worship him.”
  • Acts 7:59Stephen prays, “Lord Jesus, receive my spirit” — as Jesus entrusted his spirit to the Father (Luke 23:46).
  • 1 Cor 1:2; Rom 10:13Christians “call upon the name of our Lord Jesus Christ” (Joel 2:32).
  • Pliny, c. AD 112A hostile Roman governor reports Christians sang “a hymn to Christ, as to a god” — outside confirmation that Christians already worshipped Jesus.
A

◆ The second finger · Attributes

A
Attributes

He has what only God has by nature

Attributes are the things that are true of God by his very nature — being eternal, holding the whole universe together, knowing every heart. No single verse proves everything by itself; but together they describe one who stands on the Creator’s side of the line, not the creature’s.

“In him the whole fullness of deity dwells bodily.”Colossians 2:9 — Paul says it flatly

The evidence

  • John 1:1Eternal: “the Word was God.”
  • Colossians 1:17; Hebrews 1:3“In him all things hold together”; he “upholds the universe by the word of his power.”
  • John 2:24–25Knows every heart: he “knew what was in man.”
  • Colossians 2:9“The whole fullness of deity dwells bodily.”
N

◆ The third finger · Names

N
Names

He bears the names of God

In the Old Testament, YHWH is God’s own personal name; in Greek it was read as Kyrios, “Lord.” Again and again the New Testament takes a text about YHWH and applies it to Jesus — and in a few places calls him, outright, “God.”

“Thomas answered him, ‘My Lord and my God!’”John 20:28

The evidence

  • Philippians 2:10–11Every knee bows to Jesus in God’s own words (Isaiah 45:23).
  • Romans 10:13“Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved” (Joel 2:32).
  • 1 Peter 3:15“Honor Christ the Lord as holy” reworks Isaiah 8:13.
  • John 8:58“Before Abraham was, I am” — and they pick up stones.
  • 1 Corinthians 8:6Paul reworks the Shema — not a lesser being beside God, but the one Lord within the one God. (Bauckham & Wright; contested — McGrath reads it otherwise.)
  • Titus 2:13; 2 Peter 1:1On the most natural reading of the Greek (the grammar is debated): Titus — “our great God and Savior Jesus Christ”; 2 Peter — “our God and Savior Jesus Christ.”
D

◆ The fourth finger · Deeds

D
Deeds

He does what only God can do

Some works belong to God alone — making the world, giving life, judging every person, forgiving sins. The New Testament says Jesus does them.

“Who can forgive sins but God alone?”Mark 2:7 — the scribes, rightly

The evidence

  • John 1:3; Colossians 1:16Creation: “all things were made through him.”
  • John 5:21–22Gives life and judges — works the Old Testament keeps for God.
  • Mark 2:5–7Forgives sins: “your sins are forgiven.”
  • Matthew 24:31Gathers “his elect,” who are God’s own (Isaiah 65:9). One strand in a cumulative case, not a proof on its own.
S

◆ The fifth finger · Seat

S
Seat

He shares God’s own throne

Scripture seats Jesus on God’s own throne — and directs to that throne the undivided worship owed to God alone. The verse the New Testament quotes or alludes to most often is Psalm 110:1 — and the book of Revelation goes furthest of all.

“The LORD says to my Lord, ‘Sit at my right hand.’”Psalm 110:1

A servant may share a king’s rule — but never his worship. The Son is distinct from the Father; yet they share one throne, one name, one worship. Distinct persons, one God.

The evidence

  • Psalm 110:1“Sit at my right hand” — the Old Testament verse the NT quotes or alludes to most often.
  • Matthew 22:43–45Jesus applies it to himself.
  • Hebrews 1:13Said to no angel, ever.
  • Revelation 22:1,3“The throne of God and of the Lamb” — one seat. Revelation refuses to picture two thrones.

Five strands, written by different authors in different decades, all pointing the same way. One verse can be discussed on its own; five together point to one thing: Jesus is God — included within the very identity of the one God of Israel.

The verdict

Read in its own first-century Jewish world, the New Testament gives Jesus the Honors, Attributes, Names, Deeds, and Seat of the one God of Israel — early, widely, and on purpose.

Jesus is God.

Fully God, and fully man.

“My Lord and my God!”Thomas · John 20:28

◆ For the church

How to teach and remember

The hand drill (do it together): hold up a hand. Name each finger — Honors, Attributes, Names, Deeds, Seat. For each, say one verse. Do it three times. Now the whole congregation carries the case in their hand.
1

Which of the five fingers speaks most to you? Why?

2

Which one verse will you memorise first for each finger?

3

How does it change the way you pray and worship, to know that Jesus is God?
