Author-approved club
Rather than a cumulative "minimal facts" case, this book runs a single argument by elimination: it sets out every logically possible explanation of the resurrection reports — and of what became of Jesus' body — then works through them one by one. This club walks that argument chapter by chapter, with a few discussion questions for each session.

How the club works

Read one chapter a week and talk it over with the discussion questions — that's the whole club, and your book is all that's needed. The "Get the book" button points wherever you choose. Because this club is built with you, it carries no "independent / not endorsed" note: it's your book, in your argument's own order.
